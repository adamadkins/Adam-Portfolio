The right spacing. A sentence that makes sense. A menu that feels natural on your phone. A contact button exactly where you need it.

01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE STUDIO

Hi, I’m Adam.

Designer. Developer.
A detail person.

I like figuring out how things work, then finding ways to make them work better.

My background is in information technology. It taught me to ask questions, look beyond the obvious problem, and think about the person on the other side of the screen. I bring that same mindset to website design.

The Little Things Studios is where that practical side meets the creative one. I care about how a website looks, how it behaves, and whether it helps someone do what they came to do.

I’m the person designing and building your website. You work directly with me, from the first conversation through the final details. That keeps the conversation clear and the work personal.

I enjoy working with businesses, brands, and people who care about what they do. My job is to help that care come through online.

02 / WHAT MATTERS HERE

Good design should
make things easier.

01Clarity comes first.
Your visitors should understand what you do, find what they need, and know where to go next. Every page starts with that purpose.
02It should feel like you.
The words, colors, and layout should belong to your business. I take time to understand what makes it yours before deciding how it should look.
03The build matters, too.
Responsive layouts, usable navigation, and careful implementation are part of the design. A website should feel considered wherever someone opens it.

03 / WORKING TOGETHER

A clear process.
A shared direction.

You bring the knowledge of your business. I bring the design and development. We make the important decisions together.

  1. FIRST, WE TALK

    Understand the idea.

    We discuss your goals, the people you want to reach, and what your website needs to do. I review the project and confirm scope and pricing before work begins.

  2. THEN, WE BUILD

    Give it shape.

    I turn that direction into a website you can review. We work through feedback and refine the content, layout, and interactions together.

  3. FINALLY, WE LAUNCH

    Get the details right.

    We check the website, prepare for launch, and agree on the next steps for its care. Support options and responsibilities are set out in your project agreement.

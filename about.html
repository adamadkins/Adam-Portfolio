THE LITTLE THINGS STUDIOS / MY STORY

It started with
a little gallery.

Before there was a studio, there was a website full of photos of me and Hailie.

I got into building websites in college. My first project was a photo gallery of the two of us, back when we were still dating. She’s my wife now, and that little gallery was where this all began.

01 / A LITTLE BACKGROUND

Adam Adkins.

I grew up in Flatwoods, Kentucky, and graduated from Shawnee State University with a bachelor’s degree in Information Systems Management.

College gave me my start in building websites. The Little Things Studios became the next chapter in October 2026, with a name that goes back much further.

02 / SOMETHING MY DAD ALWAYS SAID

A saying that
stayed with me.

When I was a kid, my dad always told me:

“The difference between good and great is the little things.”

That’s where the name comes from. These days, I use that saying as an excuse to spend a little too long on the details.

It’s often the things you wouldn’t really notice on their own that tie everything together. That thought has stayed with me since childhood, and now it has a place in the name of my business.

03 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Local businesses.
A shared outlook.

I want to work with local businesses that care about the little things, too.

If that sounds like you and your business, I’d like to hear what you have in mind.

What lifetime support covers

For the original delivered website, lifetime support includes troubleshooting, bug fixes, minor text and image changes, and reasonable compatibility and maintenance updates for as long as the original website remains in service and Adam Adkins continues to provide website services. It does not include a redesign, major new pages or features, a replacement website, third-party subscriptions, hosting, domains, or services outside the original project scope. Support is provided on a reasonable-effort basis, without guaranteed response times. Details are confirmed in the project agreement before work begins.

The Little Things Studios Client Portal

The Client Portal is the private workspace we give every website design client of The Little Things Studios, a small web design studio in Kentucky. It is how a client follows their project from first preview to launch.

What the portal is for

  • Follow and approve your website. See each preview, leave feedback, and approve the launch.
  • Invoices and files. View and pay invoices and find the files we share with you.
  • Your website address. Choose and connect the domain people will type to find you.
  • Connect your services. Add the services your website uses, such as your Google Business Profile, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Square or Instagram, so we can keep your website in step with them.

How Google access works

When a client connects a Google service, Google asks them to approve one specific permission for that one service. We use it only to power the website feature they chose, for example keeping their opening hours current from their Google Business Profile. A client can disconnect at any time. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

The portal is for clients of The Little Things Studios. Access is by invitation, and clients sign in with a one-time code sent to their email. Interested in a website? Get in touch.

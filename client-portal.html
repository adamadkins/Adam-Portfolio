The Little Things Studios Client Portal

The Client Portal is a web application from The Little Things Studios, a web design studio. It gives each of our website design clients a private workspace to follow their project from the first preview to launch, and to connect the online services their website relies on. It is available at portal.tlt-studios.com for clients of the studio.

What the Client Portal does

  • Project progress. Clients see each preview of their website, leave feedback, and approve the launch.
  • Invoices and files. Clients view and pay invoices and download files we share with them.
  • Website address. Clients choose and connect the domain name people type to find them, and keep it in their own account.
  • Hosting. Clients connect their own Cloudflare account so their website is hosted in their name.
  • Connected services. Clients can add the services their website uses, so the website stays accurate without them re-typing things.

Why the Client Portal asks for Google access

The only reason the Client Portal connects to Google is to power features of a client's own website. A client adds one Google service at a time, and Google shows them the single permission that service needs. They can disconnect at any time.

  • Google Business Profile: keep the website's hours and business details in step with the client's Google listing.
  • Google Workspace email: verify the client's domain with Google and set up the records that deliver their email to Gmail. We cannot read their email.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): show upcoming events or availability on the client's website.
  • Google Sheets: save the client's website form responses to a spreadsheet that the app creates in their Drive.
  • Google Drive (read-only): show photos or a menu the client chooses to feature on their website.

We use Google user data only for these purposes. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Full details, including the exact permissions requested, how data is protected, and how to disconnect or delete it, are in our Privacy Policy.

How people get access

Access is by invitation from the studio. A client signs in with their email address and a one-time verification code, so there is no password to manage. The Client Portal is not a public sign-up service. This page is public so anyone can see what the application is and how it handles their data.

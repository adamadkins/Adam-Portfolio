Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Little Things Studios ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information. It covers our website at tlt-studios.com and our client application, The Little Things Studios Client Portal at portal.tlt-studios.com (the "Client Portal"). The Client Portal is a private workspace for our website design clients, where they follow their project, review and approve work, pay invoices, and choose which online services (such as their Google Business Profile) their website uses.

The Little Things Studios is an independent web design studio. You can reach us at hello@tlt-studios.com.

Information we collect

Website visitors and inquiries. If you send a project inquiry, we receive what you provide: your name, email address, project type, and message.

Clients. When someone becomes a client we receive their name, email address, and business details such as business name, type of business, address, phone number, current website, and team size. Clients enter some of these details themselves in the Client Portal. We also keep project files, preview feedback, and billing records.

Information from connected services. If a client chooses to connect a service such as Google, Square or Instagram, we receive limited information from that service, described in "Google user data we access" and "Other connected services" below. A client connects a service only by approving access on that service's own sign-in page; we never see or store their password for it.

Technical data. Our hosting and security providers receive ordinary request information such as IP address, browser and device information, and access logs.

Google user data we access

The Client Portal offers Google connections so we can keep a client's website in step with their Google services. Each Google service is a separate connection and asks for one specific permission. A client sees and approves only the permission for the service they are adding. We request the narrowest permission that does the job.

  • Google Business Profile, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage. We access the client's business listing information (such as name, address, phone number, opening hours and categories) so their website shows details that match their listing, and so we can keep hours and details in step.
  • Google Workspace email, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/siteverification, plus the account's email address (openid and email). We use this to verify the client's domain with Google and to set up the DNS records that deliver their email to Gmail. We do not request access to read, send or manage email, and we cannot read it.
  • Google Calendar, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly. We read the client's calendar events (read-only) so their website can show upcoming events or their availability. We never change the calendar.
  • Google Sheets, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. We create a spreadsheet in the client's Google Drive and add the responses from their website's forms to it. This permission only lets us see files our app created.
  • Google Drive, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. We read files the client chooses to feature, such as photos or a menu, so they can be shown on their website. We never change or delete files.

For each connection we also store the connected account's email address and name so the client can see which account is linked.

How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide or improve the specific website feature the client asked for, as described above. We do not use it for any other purpose, for advertising, for profiling, or to build marketing lists. We do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information or Google user data. We do not share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties. Content a client chooses to feature (for example their opening hours or photos) is displayed on that client's own website as part of the feature they requested. We disclose information to service providers only as needed to run our services, as described under "Service providers", or when required by law or to protect the security of our services. No person at our studio reads Google user data except when it is necessary to provide support the client asked for, to keep our systems secure, or to comply with the law.

How we store and protect information

Access credentials for connected services are encrypted before they are stored, are tied to the individual client and service, are never sent to the client's browser, and are never written to our logs. Access to the Client Portal requires a one-time verification code sent to the client's email address, provided through Cloudflare Access. Data is stored with Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider.

Limited Use disclosure

The Little Things Studios' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connected services

Clients may also connect Square (to show their products and prices on their website), Instagram (to show their latest posts), and Stripe (so we can add payment or donation buttons to their website that pay into the client's own Stripe account). We never see or store card numbers: payments are handled by Stripe and go to the client. The same principles apply: we request limited access, use it only for the feature the client chose, protect the credentials as described above, and do not sell the data.

Service providers

Our website and Client Portal use Cloudflare for hosting, security, database storage, authentication, and Email Sending. We use Stripe to process invoice payments, and we do not store full payment card numbers. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and have their own privacy practices. Previews of past website projects may load content from third-party domains, and visiting them may involve requests to those websites.

Cookies

Our public website does not set cookies. The Client Portal uses a sign-in cookie from Cloudflare Access to keep a signed-in client signed in, and our payment provider, Stripe, may use cookies on its own hosted payment page. These are necessary for sign-in, security and payments. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Data retention and deletion

We keep inquiry and project information for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, billing records, and legal obligations. Credentials for a connected service are kept only while it stays connected. When a client disconnects a service in the Client Portal, we revoke our access with the provider where it offers that option and delete the stored credentials. To ask us to delete other information we hold about you, or to correct it, email hello@tlt-studios.com or use our contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Your choices and controls

A client can disconnect any service at any time from Integrations in the Client Portal. A Google user can also remove our access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can ask for a copy of, correction of, or deletion of the information we hold about you at any time using the contact details below.

Children

Our website and Client Portal are intended for business owners and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or services change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version. If we change what Google user data we access or how we use it, we will update this page before the change takes effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hello@tlt-studios.com or use our contact page.

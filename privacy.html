Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Information we receive

If you send a project inquiry, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address, project type, and message. We may also receive business contact or billing information when you become a client.

How we use it

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, provide website services, manage client projects, send invoices, and keep our website and client portal functioning securely.

Services involved

Our website uses Cloudflare for hosting and security, FormSubmit to deliver project inquiries to hello@tlt-studios.com through our email routing, and Stripe for eligible invoice payments. Our separate client portal uses Cloudflare Access for authentication and Cloudflare Email Sending for optional client invitations. These providers process information as needed to deliver their respective services and have their own privacy practices.

Previews of past website projects may load content from third-party domains. Visiting those previews or opening external project links may involve requests to those websites.

Technical data and cookies

Website and security providers can receive ordinary request information, such as IP address, browser and device information, and access logs. Authentication and payment services may use cookies or similar technology necessary for their functions.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry and project information for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, billing records, and applicable obligations. If you want to ask about information you submitted, or request a correction or deletion where appropriate, please use our contact page.

Updates

We may update this notice as our website or services change. The date above identifies the latest published version.
